The travel poster of SELENARA — a place that isn't there. A giant
moon has fallen out of the sky and come to rest half-submerged in a
turquoise sea at sunset: a lone figure in white stands on the rim of
the moon, a single sailboat drifts on the calm water below, and a
path of golden light leads the eye from the moon to the horizon.
Warm sunset gold, teal sea, vintage poster grain — one impossible
landmark, one clear promise of a stay.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only) copies the finished 1024x1024
poster `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png` — the poster shows the
giant moon resting in the sea, a lone figure in white standing on
its rim, a sailboat below, and a golden light path on the teal
water, framed in cream like a vintage poster, with the name
SELENARA in huge cream serif and the tagline "WHERE THE MOON CAME
TO REST" beneath it. No generation, no network, no randomness:
two runs produce the same bytes.
