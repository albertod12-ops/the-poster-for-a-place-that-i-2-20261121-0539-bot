The travel poster of MOONFALL — a place that isn't there. A giant
moon rests half-submerged in a turquoise bay at sunset: a luminous
waterfall pours out of a low golden cloud, falls onto the moon's
rim, and cascades off the moon into the sea in a sheet of gold-lit
water — waterfall and moon are one landmark. A lone figure in white
stands on the moon's rim where the waterfall lands, a whitewashed
cliff village climbs the rock on the right, and a single sailboat
drifts on the calm water below a path of golden light. Warm sunset
gold, teal sea, vintage poster grain — one impossible landmark, one
clear promise of a stay.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only) copies the finished 1024x1024
poster `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png` — the poster shows the
giant moon in the bay, the waterfall falling from a golden cloud
onto the moon and cascading into the sea, a lone figure in white on
the moon's rim, a white village on the cliff and a sailboat below,
framed in cream like a vintage poster, with the name MOONFALL in
huge cream serif arched along the moon and the tagline "WHERE THE
MOON POURS INTO THE SEA" beneath it. No generation, no network, no
randomness: two runs produce the same bytes.
