The travel poster of ONDINA — a place that isn't there. A colossal
nautilus shell rises out of a turquoise sea at sunset: the name
ONDINA is carved huge into the shell's curving flank, steaming
thermal pools fill the shell's great opening with bathers, a small
whitewashed village clings to the shell's rim, and a single sailboat
drifts on the calm water. Warm sunset gold, teal sea, vintage poster
grain — one impossible landmark, one clear promise of a stay.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only) copies the finished 1024x1024
poster `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png` — the poster shows the
colossal nautilus shell in the sea, the name ONDINA carved huge
into the shell, bathers in the steaming pools inside it, a white
village on its rim and a sailboat below, framed in cream like a
vintage poster, with the tagline "WHERE THE SEA KEEPS ITS WARMTH"
curved beneath the name. No generation, no network, no randomness:
two runs produce the same bytes.
