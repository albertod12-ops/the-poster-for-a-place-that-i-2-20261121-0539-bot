The travel poster of MOONFALL — a place that isn't there. A
colossal moon fills the background sky behind a turquoise bay at
sunset: the name MOONFALL arches huge across the moon in cream
serif, and below it a whitewashed cliff village climbs terraced
rock down to the water — lit windows, balconies, stairways, pine
trees, people on the terraces, and a small harbour with boats. A
single sailboat drifts on the calm water, and a path of golden
light leads across the bay toward the moon and the horizon. Warm
sunset gold, teal sea, vintage poster grain — one impossible
landmark, one clear promise of a stay.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only) copies the finished 1024x1024
poster `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png` — the poster shows the
colossal moon behind the bay, the name MOONFALL arched huge across
it, and the white cliff village climbing down to the harbour below,
with one sailboat on the water, framed in cream like a vintage
poster, with the tagline "WHERE THE MOON POURS INTO THE SEA"
beneath the name. No generation, no network, no randomness: two
runs produce the same bytes.
